    Hear and see how the factory is running

    This is a simulated factory day: materials arrive, three assembly lines build products, QA inspects them, and trucks collect them from the warehouse.

    Rather than reading numbers, let your built-in pattern recognition do the work. After a while you'll learn what a normal day looks and sounds like, and notice when something changes.

    • Listen: roughly harmonious, flowing music means things are fine. Discordant, off-key or off-beat sound means something isn't quite right.
    • Look: each coloured thread is one measurement; wider means more. Newest is at the top. Red dots are alarms.
    • Explore: inject faults or change rates on the right, and toggle homeostasis to see whether the factory recovers on its own.

    Sound is off until you turn it on with 🔈 Enable sound in the top bar. Headphones or speakers help; the sound is panned left to right along the flow.
